1. The list of tables, with columns, and keys, resulting from translating the ER model to relational model;
STUDENT: student_id PK, gender 

CONTROLLABLES: student_id PK, FK references STUDENT(student_id), 
               assignment_completion_rate, study_hours_per_day, self_study_hours, online_learning_hours,
               study_consistency, study_method, revision_frequency, practice_tests_done, sleep_hours, daily_screen_time, physical_activity_hours

PERFORMANCE: student_id PK, FK references STUDENT(student_id), 
             previous_exam_score, prep_days, exam_score, performance_grade, pass_status

UNCONTROLLABLES: student_id PK, FK references STUDENT(student_id),
                 family_income, parent_education, school_type, urban_rural, internet_access
