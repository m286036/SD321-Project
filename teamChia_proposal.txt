Project Title: Student Performance Factors 
Thesis Question: How can you optimize controllable factors to increase academic performance.

Dataset: Student Exam Performance & Success Dataset
            -This csv file contains information about both high school and undergraduate students. It highlights their parents backgrounds as well as their own, and how this is
             impacting school performance. What is most interesting for our group is diving deeper into the uncontrollable/controllable factors like: number_of_practice_exams_taken,
             motivation_level, attendence_percentage, and many more. With 44 columns and 100,000 rows we are excited to uncover the secrets behind student performance.


link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mobeenfatimah/student-exam-performance-and-success-dataset

How does parents background impact student performance?

how is motivation level measured?
